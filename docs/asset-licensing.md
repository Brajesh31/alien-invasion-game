## Verification Required

Before creating a binary release or redistributing the repository as a bundled game:
1. Identify the original source of each asset.
2. Confirm the asset license or written permission.
3. Record the required attribution, notice, and redistribution conditions.
4. Keep any required notices with the relevant asset or distribution.
5. Replace any asset whose redistribution rights cannot be verified.

Do not invent authors, origins, or license terms. Asset provenance was not established by the source-file inventory alone during this maintenance pass.

## Project License Boundary

The root `LICENSE` file contains an MIT License attributed to Brajesh Kumar. That project license does not automatically supersede different terms attached to bundled fonts, images, music, or sound effects. Review the terms of each asset independently.

## Future Maintenance

When adding or replacing an asset, update [Asset Inventory](assets.md), record verified provenance, and include a concise explanation in the pull request. Keep large generated or unrelated media out of the repository.

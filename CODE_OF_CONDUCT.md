# 🌱 Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The Alien Invasion Game project welcomes constructive participation. Everyone involved in issues, pull requests, reviews, and project discussions is expected to help maintain a respectful and useful environment.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate respectfully and in good faith.
- Critique the code or proposal, not the person.
- Keep feedback specific, actionable, and relevant.
- Be open to corrections and differing technical opinions.
- Respect privacy and personal boundaries.
- Give credit for work and assets where attribution is required and verified.
- Keep project spaces focused on improving the game and its documentation.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks, insults, or repeated bad-faith disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without consent.
- Sharing credentials, private files, or sensitive information.
- Deliberately sabotaging project discussions or contributions.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers may remove content that violates this policy and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community. Actions should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to Brajesh Kumar (`@Brajesh31`) through a private GitHub contact channel. Include links and relevant context where safe. Avoid sharing unrelated personal information.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, and other spaces where a person represents or participates in the project.

## Acknowledgement

Participation in the project implies agreement to follow this policy and cooperate with reasonable maintainer requests intended to keep project spaces safe and constructive.
